Privacy Policy

Prayer List for iPhone and Android

SupportPrivacy Policy

Effective November 25, 2026

Prayer List is made by Adam Mott. Your prayers are personal, so the app is designed to keep them that way. Prayer List does not collect, store, sell, or share any of your personal information. There are no accounts, no advertising, and no analytics or tracking.

What stays on your phone

Everything you enter in Prayer List is saved only on your device, including your prayer requests, details and categories, which days you've prayed, answered-prayer notes, and your reminder times. This information is never sent to us or to anyone else. Deleting the app deletes this data. If you back up your phone (with iCloud or a computer on iPhone, or with Google's device backup on Android), the app's data may be included in that backup under Apple's or Google's own privacy terms.

Contacts

If you use “Text them when I pray,” you choose a person with your phone's built-in contact picker. The app sees only the contact you select, never your whole address book, and does not ask for permission to read your contacts. It saves that person's name and phone number with the request on your phone. When you check the request off, the app prepares a text message in your phone's messaging app. The message is sent only if you tap Send, and it goes through your own messaging service, not through us.

Notifications

Prayer reminders and the reminders you set for people on your list are scheduled on your phone by the app itself. No notification data passes through any server. You can turn reminders off in the app or in your phone's Settings at any time.

Verse of the day

To show the daily verse, the app requests that day's passage (for example, “Psalm 23:1”) from the ESV API run by Crossway, the publisher of the English Standard Version. The request contains only the passage reference, nothing about you or your prayer list. As with any internet request, Crossway's servers receive your device's IP address. See Crossway's ESV.org privacy policy for how they handle it. Only today's verse is kept on your device.

Children

Prayer List does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children under 13.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
