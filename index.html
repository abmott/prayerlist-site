Common questions

Where is my prayer list stored?
Only on your phone. There is no account and nothing is uploaded. If you delete the app, its data is deleted too.
Why am I not getting prayer reminders?
Open the Reminders tab and make sure a prayer time is switched on. Then make sure notifications are allowed for Prayer List: on iPhone, Settings → Notifications → Prayer List; on Android, Settings → Apps → Prayer List → Notifications. On Android, also allow “Alarms & reminders” for the app so reminders arrive on time.
The verse of the day isn't showing.
The verse text is loaded from the internet once a day. Check your connection and reopen the app. The passage reference is shown even when offline.
How does “Text them when I pray” work?
When you add a request, you can choose someone from your contacts. After you check that request off, Prayer List opens a ready-to-send message for you. Nothing is sent unless you tap Send yourself.
